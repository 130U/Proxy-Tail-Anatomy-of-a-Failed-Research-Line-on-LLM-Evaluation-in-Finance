# Round 5: historical non-interference and the admission of real sources must be proved separately

Date 2026-11-16; local branch research/certifiable, base commit 8e4dd0b33c95ac2ac1a6015eba6f2dfafe5c1254. The new outputs are not committed yet. The whole round was offline. There were no outside messages, no reading of private data and no main-line model training.

## 1. The most important conclusion of this round

**"Future appends do not change past outputs", "the output is measurable with respect to historical information" and "the documents used were actually available at the time" are three different obligations.** We implemented the complete preprocessing chain for the first one and checked it adversarially, and we wrote out the conditional proof of the second one. Neither can replace the third. When 3 wrong timestamps were planted in the synthetic world, all 3 entered the historical snapshot, and the future-append check still passed. This makes clear that the weakest link of P1 is still the authenticity of sources and times, not the lack of a larger model.

In addition, the independent review corrected an old theoretical statement. "The seed is independent of the future marginal" can still let the seed and the history together leak the future; what is needed is conditional independence given the history. It also pointed out that a checkpoint fixed after the fact is measurable as a constant, and that this is not a proof of historical constructibility. Both conceptual corrections have been added to the main theory notes and to the new draft.

## 3. Corrections to the advisor's hypotheses

The advisor's WP1.2 treats the future-append test as a check of the constructive certificate. This round keeps it as necessary regression evidence, and at the same time states clearly that finite tests cannot prove non-interference over all inputs. A conclusion over all inputs depends on a closed input construction and on implementation review. Arbitrarily corrupted event headers are also outside the theorem's input domain, and the result cannot be stretched into a guarantee of secure service availability.

The historical snapshot is projected by knowledge_time, and then versions and tombstones are replayed. The original release date/record_time can be kept in a revision, but it cannot let a new version enter an old snapshot early. Labels, source/core and control metadata must also be known at that point in time. The historical exogeneity of the configuration/seed is still an external premise; writing it as a constant does not satisfy it automatically.

For the real local material: in the earlier fixed sample of 10 FOMC documents, 3/6/10 respectively claim release before the end of 2015/2020/2025. The number that claim local acquisition before these points is 0 in each case, and the number with a proven historical byte version is also 0 in each case. This shows the existing evidence gap. It cannot be used to infer that the content was actually unavailable at the time, still less to estimate the population mislabel rate.

## 4. Decision gates and items that need Fable's judgment

WP1.2 moved from "no construction" to "a six-component reference implementation + conditional proofs + independent checks". The authenticity of the real sources, the shared dependencies of the full model and the final repository migration are still not done. G0/G1 remain failed; this round did not bypass the gates to train P1/P2 models. The investment ranking of the five paths did not change automatically, and the existing mathematical results on the P1 audit structure still need an independent novelty judgment.

A new question for Fable that needs to be settled first: **does the flagship certify a model that is constructible ex ante, the per-version admission of historical sources, or a fixed comparator statistic after contamination is removed?** The three can form a chain, but they cannot be swapped under each other's names. If the real historical sources cannot be verified for now, should the near-term paper question be narrowed explicitly to a bound relating audit information to statistic width for a given impact structure, with the complete historical certificate kept as a condition for application? This is a revision to be discussed, not a flagship already chosen on the advisor's behalf.

This round's internal review is different from feedback from Fable, and the materials were not sent out. The user does not need to provide SEC contact details; neither this round nor the later independent mathematical work depends on outside contact.

## 5. Next steps

Priority goes to continuing falsifiable mathematical questions: cost lower bounds and strong controls for the audit structure, or the necessary width of the output envelope induced by P2 parsing ambiguity. Full model training first needs a new, explicit G0 substitution protocol and a non-redundant task consistent with the certificate target. Adding more small PIT examples that pass will not by itself resolve source authenticity or originality.

## 2. Key judgements changed by the adversarial review

**The main example cannot prove that positional modelling is necessary.** The [independent paper review](paper_review.md) points out that under balanced quotas, the simple total-contamination-count certificate K≤r−1 already reaches the main example's 88 audits and the Ω(r) separation relative to the rectangle. This is because the per-stratum zero-finding probability satisfies p_h(q)≤(1−n/N)^q; one contamination in each of any r strata attains the bound. Given this, the full text must state clearly that the original theorem isolates the cost of the rectangle's intermediate commitment.

**A general count confidence set can be equivalent to the direct method.** Take the region D of the legal count grid that dominates no minimal bad count vector; report D when all findings are zero, and the full space otherwise. Its worst-case miscoverage probability equals that of the direct method. This result does not overturn the existing rectangle theorem, but it rules out the broader reading "better than all count confidence methods".

**The value of position needs a different comparison.** In an example derived in post-review exploration (three strata of 20 documents each, bad only if two strata are affected, α=.05), the direct method's optimum is 38; the optimum of the fixed-stratified total K≤1 gate is 48. If any stratum audits fewer than 16 documents, placing two contaminations in that stratum makes the miss risk exceed .05; auditing 16 per stratum reaches risk 1/25, so the lower bound of 48 is attainable. This comparison applies only to the explicit total-count gate, and is not a lower bound for all count methods.

The independent review also used a [reproducible script](paper_review_checks.py) to enumerate all 9,261 quotas and 26 small balanced designs, checking the comparison above. These are mathematical soundness checks, not new confirmatory model experiments.

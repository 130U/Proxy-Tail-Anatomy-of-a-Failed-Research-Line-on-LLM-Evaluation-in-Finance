Date: 2026-11-15 · Internet access: verified first, then stopped · Status: statistical methods partly complete; corpus facts not complete.

## 1. Conclusion

Hypergeometric inversion at a fixed sample size suits uniform sampling without replacement for auditing a finite corpus. Sequential audits already have time-uniform confidence sequences that allow valid early stopping, but this does not mean a free improvement on every fixed-sample bound. The rule of three is an approximation for zero findings and a low sampling fraction; it is not a lower bound on contamination already observed. A deterministic influence bound and an audit upper bound can be chained directly; they do not need to be independent.

## 4. Minimal viable test

Fix W, N, the sample size n and the criterion for a wrong label in advance. X follows Hyp(N,K,n); define K_u(x)=max{K: P_K(X≤x)>α}. This is an upper bound with a coverage guarantee over the audit randomness; it needs the manual verification to be the truth, and if the auditor misses cases, those errors must be counted separately. For x=0, use the ratio of binomial coefficients rather than an approximation to the exact finite-population inversion; for n=N, K_u=x, and the rule of three does not apply.

Conditional on K≤K_u, if the deterministic certificate is valid for every possible contamination set, the final interval for a fixed test statistic has coverage ≥1−α. Several statistical steps whose independence is unknown can be combined by adding their failure probabilities; the union bound does not depend on independence. If the trusted core is also only audited by sampling, a single contaminated item in the core may change every head, so the core K and the periphery K cannot simply be added and then used with the periphery partition bound.

Stratifying by source requires specifying the strata in advance, sampling uniformly within each, allocating α_h, and then adding the K upper bounds of the strata. Non-uniform convenience sampling cannot be plugged directly into Hyp. Repeatedly checking the fixed-n confidence bound and stopping when it looks good breaks coverage; use the confidence sequences of L08, or allocate the error budget across observation times in advance.

## 5. Veto conditions

If the criterion for a wrong label has no verifiable "when was this version of the content first publicly available", the audit variable itself is undefined. If the labellers' accuracy is unknown and cannot be measured, stop treating K_u as an upper bound on the true number of mislabels.

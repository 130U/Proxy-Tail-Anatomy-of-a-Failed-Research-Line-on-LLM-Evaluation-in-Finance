# Q10: Certifiable monotonicity and Model Greeks

Date: 2026-11-15 · Internet access: verified first, then stopped at the user's request · Status: partly complete.

## 1. Conclusion

The monotone structure "Lipschitz network + linear residual" was proposed by Kitouni et al. and cannot be this project's original theorem. Attaching it to a text representation isolated from the numbers can give a conditional certificate, but when the numbers leak back into the context through embeddings, unit words, comparative expressions, gating or thresholds, the conclusion may fail. Calling it Greeks creates no mathematical novelty. A certificate also does not mean the output has correct financial semantics.

## 4. Minimal viable test

Fix c and ordered thresholds θ, and let η(g,c)=h(g,c)+λΣ(r∈M)g_r, where h is λ-Lipschitz in g under ℓ1. For r∈M, the difference form gives 0≤η(g+te_r,c)−η(g,c)≤2λt (t≥0); where differentiable, the same derivative bound holds. The derivative of the top-class probability of the cumulative logit lies in [0,λ/2]. This is a direct derivation from the existing structure and is not claimed as an independent new theorem. FOSD is guaranteed only when the M coordinates increase and the rest are held fixed; non-monotone coordinates cannot vary freely at the same time.

Counterexample: even if h is bounded in its first argument g, with c(g)=−100g and h(g,c)=c the total output can decrease. Replacing only the Arabic numerals while keeping their written-out form also does not guarantee isolation. The implementation should check that thresholds, routing, normalization, context caches and data parsing have no hidden dependence on g. The real-number theorem and the floating-point computation checks are reported separately.

## 5. Veto conditions

If the target is the direction of a fully extracted growth rate, a threshold rule can answer it, and the need to train a language model is withdrawn. If a reasonable monotone semantic intervention cannot be defined, switch to a Lipschitz-sensitivity-only guarantee; do not force stance monotonicity.

## 6. Uncertainties

There are no results on this project's real text yet, not all monotonicity work on language models has been covered, and not all of LHCb's speed figures have been verified. Same-data comparisons are needed: purely symbolic, ordinal regression, a frozen-encoder hard head, soft constraints, and unconstrained.

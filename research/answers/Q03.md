# Q03: Model-level look-ahead freedom and contamination shift bounds

Date: 2026-11-15 · Internet access: yes for the first half of the round, stopped after the user's restriction · Status: partly complete.

## 1. Conclusion

A non-interference formalization of look-ahead freedom already exists. Model robustness under residual mislabelling and intervals for fixed statistics remain a researchable connection, but this round is not enough to claim it is a first. Fonseca's paper explicitly does not check model weights, and lists bringing the pre-training cutoff date into the certifiable properties as future work. DPA already gives prediction-invariance bounds when a few training samples change. P1 must control the entire training dependency graph; it cannot just add a date filter to the corpus.

## 4. Minimal viable test

Fix the set W of suspect documents, and assume that the correct core, the tokenizer, the preprocessing, the hyperparameters and the randomness are not changed by contamination in W. Let the clean comparator be the model retrained with the same algorithm and the same randomness after removing, from the observed training set, the documents that are truly later than the cutoff date. If deleting K documents affects only K heads, then for any fixed test set, lower and upper bounds on a bounded per-item statistic can be obtained from the set of labels reachable at each point. Substituting an upper confidence bound on K from an audit without replacement gives an interval that is valid for the audit sampling.

First it must be formalized whether "changing a document" may change its ID: if changing the ID moves the document across partitions, one modification can affect two partitions. Shared backbones, global normalization, cross-partition early stopping and similar paths must be excluded from the dependency paths of the suspect window. Future test labels are used only for post-hoc evaluation and must not enter training selection.

If DP is at the document level and the object of comparison is a bounded statistic, its group privacy can bound the change in the distribution/expectation averaged over training randomness; this does not automatically become an interval for the clean statistic of one given run. A theory task must give a separate rigorous statement. This round did not verify the ε/μ achievable by ≤50M from-scratch DP pre-training, and does not substitute the advisor's expectation for a measurement.

## 5. Veto conditions

If existing work covers the same temporal sources, residual-mislabelling audit and model-statistic intervals, withdraw the claim of an original combination. If core contamination can affect all heads, the current partition proof does not hold, and the architecture must be fixed or the conclusion rewritten first.

## 6. Uncertainties

Certified unlearning, algorithmic stability and all 2023–2026 LM poisoning work have not been tracked systematically; Fonseca's full soundness and undecidability proofs have not been independently reviewed. There is no non-empty certificate on a real corpus; G1 cannot be passed.

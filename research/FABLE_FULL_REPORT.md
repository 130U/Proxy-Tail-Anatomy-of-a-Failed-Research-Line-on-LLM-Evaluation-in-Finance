## 1. Research motivation: from training a small model to proving a conclusion

The problem with the old project was not mainly that the model was small. It was that the project did not prove its design was irreplaceable: if existing lexical methods, frozen encoders or symbolic rules can answer the same question, retraining a financial classifier does not by itself constitute a theoretical contribution. The shift the advisor proposed was to carry over the idea from the user's earlier financial mathematics, "give a proven interval for a quantity that cannot be observed directly", to model research, without treating parameter count or system complexity as the result.

The central object is the **certificate chain**. The construction step guarantees that the model, the data dependence or the output shape satisfies stated conditions. The statistical step turns finite audit information into a statement with coverage. The bound step propagates the remaining uncertainty to the final statistic. The strength of the chain is set by its weakest link. Proving a monotone head does not prove that parsing is correct; the existence of a time field does not prove that the historical content was available; zero findings in a sample do not prove zero contamination in the population. Failure probabilities can be combined over valid events, but "the code tests passed" cannot be assigned zero failure probability at will. [Foundational theory](theory/notes.md)

The two original main lines were these. P1 certifies a fixed comparator statistic after contamination is removed, and discusses historical no-look-ahead only when source conditions are additionally satisfied. P2 guarantees unit invariance, direction and sensitivity through a numeric channel and an ordered head. Audit design, deployment tail risk and checkable numeric programs are candidates developed around these two lines. Over nine rounds the work gradually found that many basic components have prior work. What is most worth discussing is not whether they can be combined, but which condition in the combination is genuinely verifiable, which component is necessary, and what the strongest simple method still cannot solve.

## 3. Five path reports: four families, no flagship selected yet

**Path 01: audit-driven information-set certificates.** The goal is to turn the remaining contamination constraint into an interval for clean-comparator accuracy or macro-F1. In form this is closest to the research on financial price bounds. It must face alternatives such as core-only training, per-document review, and fixed lexical features plus simple shard heads. Once the shared core, the vocabulary, the selection rule or the metadata is affected by future information, deleting edge documents still cannot restore the historical model. Random initialization is one way to control dependence, not the logically unique implementation; an authoritative, historically frozen encoder might also satisfy the conditions. The biggest obstacles remain source admission, the real model chain and the difference from prior work. [Path 01 full text](paths/PATH_01_audited_information_set.md)

**Path 02: numeric sensitivity under parsing uncertainty.** Once the correct tuple is given, unit invariance and monotonicity can be guaranteed by hard structure. The real difficulty is whether entity, metric, period, unit and baseline are bound correctly. If the label is only sign(x−b), a sign rule already solves the problem and a learned head is unnecessary. What is worth keeping is semantic set coverage, necessary width and post-acceptance risk, not keeping the model first and then looking for reasons to justify it. For probabilistic learning tasks, an independent random target and a direction specification are also needed. [Path 02 full text](paths/PATH_02_numeric_sensitivity.md)

**Path 03: audit cost vs. certificate target.** Given the impact structure, the target interval, the error budget and the allowed sampling class, it asks for the minimum amount of audit information needed. It has produced the most complete conditional mathematical results, but the simple total-count gate and the randomized whole-stratum strategy keep shrinking the range in which "a complex method is necessary". This is a mathematical branch of 01 and should not be counted again as a second flagship. If the difference is only a direct combination of mature statistical tools, it should be kept as a P1 component. [Path 03 full text](paths/PATH_03_audit_design_frontier.md)

**Path 04: deployment risk under marginal and dependence uncertainty.** The question is not how to plug calibrated scores into a tail formula, but which observable information can identify the future joint-error structure. Under arbitrary dependence, the upper-tail risk can reach the full batch size, and a larger model cannot make up for missing identification conditions. It is close to the user's existing research on risk bounds, but its relation to "must train from scratch" is the weakest, and there is currently no applicable evidence from repeated batches. [Path 04 full text](paths/PATH_04_dependence_robust_risk.md)

**Path 05: numeric reasoning with checkable computational evidence.** Decomposing an answer into facts, unit types, a program and interval arithmetic can localize errors, but a program whose types and arithmetic are both correct can still answer the wrong question. The key question is whether the correct semantic program was left out of the candidate set. If rule extraction plus a calculator already shows the same risk–coverage performance, there is no reason to expand a language proposal model. It can become a verification tool for 02; the two share the weak parsing link and do not spread risk independently. [Path 05 full text](paths/PATH_05_proof_carrying_numeric_reasoning.md)

The current investment order is 01 and 03 merged as Top 1, 02 as Top 2, 04 as Top 3, and 05 as an exploratory alternate. The independent review gave a different ranking, 03→01→05→02→04, and the disagreement was not removed by "voting". More fundamentally, if the literal hard gate "from-scratch language training is logically irreplaceable" is upheld, none of the three paths has been shown to qualify. Whether to change it to "dependence is controllable and auditable, and there is an object that simple methods have not solved" must be decided explicitly by Fable. [Comparison and disagreements](paths/COMPARATIVE_ANALYSIS.md)

## 5. Theoretical results and their necessary assumptions

### 1. T series: correcting the meaning of the basic certificates

T0 gives the combination of failure events. T1.1 requires historical selection, labels, metadata, code and randomness to be controlled jointly. The DP comparison in T1.2 gives an expected shift and cannot be relabeled directly as an interval for a single checkpoint. T1.3 depends on fixed-ID sharding and locally deterministic training; changing an ID may affect two shards, and core contamination may affect all heads and cannot be counted like an ordinary edge document. T1.5 certifies an explicit deletion comparator; upgrading it to historical no-leakage also requires source and admission conditions.

T1.4 uses an exact hypergeometric audit. The zero-finding probability is the ratio of binomial coefficients C(N−K,n)/C(N,n). At 95%, the common approximation 3N/n expresses the audit resolution at low sampling fractions. It is neither a lower bound on the actual contamination count nor a proof of zero contamination. The original necessary condition of "at least 2k+1 heads" has tie exceptions; the actual vote margin must be examined. Failing to certify pointwise also does not mean that the final statistic interval is necessarily vacuous.

T2.1 points out that non-decreasing upper-tail probabilities for all thresholds (UP), label invariance, and a lower bound on the change in the relative log-odds between two classes (REL margin) cannot substitute for one another. The monotone direction in T2.2 applies only to the specified coordinate change with fixed text context and thresholds; the derivative bound holds almost everywhere, and the finite-increment bound holds everywhere. T2.3 requires the full-model bypass to be unit invariant as well; only complete dimensional invariants together with the positive-domain condition support the expressiveness claim, and negative numbers, zero and denominator boundaries need separate treatment. The entity-slot invariance of T2.4 depends on correct alias and role binding. T3.1 gives the comonotone worst-case convex risk and ES when the true Bernoulli marginals are known and clusters are independent; VaR does not follow this conclusion, and group calibration does not provide pointwise true marginals. [Complete proofs for the T series](theory/notes.md)

### 2. CM and IA: from shared head changes to the final statistic

CM fixes the test points, the labels and one common set of affected heads, and allows those heads to output anything at each point. For a fixed set, the accuracy extremes are attainable pointwise. Macro-F1 is non-additive: adding the per-class component extremes generally gives only a safe outer envelope, and exact optimization needs the joint confusion-matrix state. "Exact" here refers to the output-level relaxation; it does not guarantee that real retraining can realize every output. [CM proof](theory/collective_metrics.md)

IA maps each document to the set of heads it may affect. This dependence upper bound must hold simultaneously for all allowed deletion sets and cannot be inferred from per-document deletion tests. Bad configurations are upward closed, and the zero-finding risk under fixed stratification reduces to the minimal bad configurations and an antichain of stratum counts; finite enumeration gives the global optimum for the declared design class. The exact joint risk of a safe rectangle is the maximum of the per-stratum exclusion risks, not a Bonferroni sum. General non-rectangular safe count regions can in turn fully reproduce the direct method, so one cannot claim to surpass all count-based confidence methods. [IA proof](theory/impact_audit.md)

### 3. PNI and SE: historical sources and semantics are not side errors

PNI proves, on a finite canonical log space, that historical-equivalence non-interference corresponds to factorization through the historical projection. Revisions are admitted by knowledge time, not event time. Future deletions, dedup bridges, vocabularies, sampling, validation and hyperparameters may not affect old outputs. A hash of the whole future log can only stay in the audit shell; it cannot enter stable model artifacts. Fixed-seed path invariance, parameter measurability and exogenous randomness are different statements; a seed that is only independent of the future marginal can still leak through combinations of historical variables. More importantly, the same observed log can be compatible with different worlds of real availability: neither formal non-interference nor mathematical measurability automatically certifies the actual historical source. [PNI proof](theory/pit_noninterference.md)

SE gives a joint outer envelope on the probabilities for finite semantic branches under a shared target. The corner points of a positive baseline ratio are tight only on an independent rectangular input domain; dropping the coupling between variables creates conservative width. The intermediate categories of an ordered logit are not monotone, and the peak can be at the midpoint between adjacent thresholds. A candidate scalar span D forces any fully covering interval to have width at least D, and any point answer to have worst-case error at least D/2. This is a lower bound relative to the candidate specification; a parser reporting extra candidates does not prove that the source text is intrinsically unidentifiable. Only branches with fixed correspondence and the same direction inherit monotone and Lipschitz envelopes; dynamic branch selection has no global Greeks. The post-acceptance certificate failure rate does not exceed the post-acceptance parsing non-coverage risk; the overall error rate cannot be used directly as the conditional error rate. [SE proof](theory/semantic_envelopes.md)

### 4. LV and RA: the allowed audit design must be written into the conclusion

LV proves that the worst-case miss probability of the strongest fixed total-count gate can be computed exactly by a prefix greedy over each stratum's non-increasing marginal chain. Under homogeneous r-of-d structure with fixed per-stratum quotas, the cost ratio of the optimal total-count gate to the direct method is at most d/(d−r+1). For the original d=2r−1 family the ratio is below 2 and can approach 2; at the original paper's N=r² scale the ratio instead tends to 1. All-strata conjunction and star structures can give order-level gains over the total-count gate, but these gains are already attained, respectively, by a single-stratum census and by a clean rectangle gate that audits only the central stratum. [LV proof](theory/localization_value.md)

RA removes the fixed per-stratum quotas. There are d strata of N documents each; the bad set is fixed before randomization; badness means exactly that at least r strata are damaged. Exactly m distinct documents are drawn under any distribution; **if all findings are zero the pre-specified I must be reported, and if anything is found the full range is reported**. Otherwise, never issuing a certificate makes the minimum error risk degenerate to 0. Writing q=floor(m/N) and t=m−qN, for m<dN the exact optimal worst-case risk is

$$
V(m)=\frac{\binom{d-q-1}{r}+(1-t/N)\binom{d-q-1}{r-1}}{\binom dr}.
$$

The full census is listed separately, with value 0. Taking a census of q random strata and then sampling t documents in another random stratum attains the lower bound. A uniformly random minimal bad set gives the lower bound for any design, and quota compression plus random permutation prove that it is attained; this is not merely finding one good scheme. Within the same class of arbitrary randomized designs, the optimal risk of the total-count gate is C(dN−m,r)/C(dN,r), attained by uniform sampling without replacement over the whole population. Adaptivity under binary feedback, a hard budget and the all-zero must-certify rule cannot beat this value; an expected budget must constrain the clean world or all ground truths, not only the bad world. If r-of-d is only a conservative outer envelope of the actual model, the optimality also holds only for that threat family. [Complete RA proof](theory/randomized_audit.md)

## 6. Experimental results: read the real task, hand-made structures and analytic bounds separately

**Real-source task.** After strict parsing of SEntFiN and isolation of anomalies, 14,284 entity rows were kept; train/dev/test have 10,041/2,136/2,107 rows respectively, and the test set contains 1,570 lexical clusters. The task is negative/neutral/positive sentiment toward a specified entity in a headline, not investment stance. Near-duplicates were grouped transitively, and then a non-temporal hash split was made. TF-IDF was fitted only on the training set, and C was selected only on the dev set.

| System | Test macro-F1 | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Majority class | 0.1813 | Basic diagnostic |
| word TF-IDF + LR | 0.7920 | Newly trained simple baseline |
| char TF-IDF + LR | 0.7548 | Pre-registered main comparison |
| entity-only TF-IDF + LR | 0.5336 | Identity-shortcut diagnostic |

The pre-registered char−word difference is −0.037233, and the nominal 95% interval from 10,000 paired lexical-cluster bootstrap resamples is [−0.053059,−0.021560]. It is conditional on the training and dev selection already carried out, and lexical clusters do not guarantee that real events are independent; this is an approximate resampling description, not a certificate. The entity-only score cannot be decomposed into an "identity-explained share". The new task cannot be compared directly with the old 0.6549, and FinBERT or small-LLM scores have not been added. [Training aggregate](experiments/real_baseline/baseline_summary.json), [interval aggregate](experiments/real_baseline/bootstrap_summary.json)

**Synthetic foundation and component negative results.** In the four-configuration × three-seed ablation of a 13,739-parameter model, the macro-F1 difference permissions−edited_ce was 0 in every case; a simple edited CE already removes the violations observed in this example. The [0,0] interval from a saturated test cannot prove that the population difference is zero, and shuffling the permissions also changes the composition of the effective gradient, so the result cannot be attributed to a pure semantic effect. The synthetic world passes checks of accounting identities, exact contamination counts and same-seed repeatability, but it has only finite templates and does not represent natural financial text. [Foundation results](experiments/local_foundations/report.md)

For hand-made audit inputs at α=.05 with zero findings and (N,n) equal to (100000,1000), (10000,1000) and (1000,500), the exact contamination upper bounds are 297, 28 and 4 respectively. The CM shared-head example tightens accuracy from [0,1] to [1/4,3/4]. In another example the macro-F1 component envelope [4/9,2/3] is in fact exactly the single point 5/9, which shows that a non-additive target cannot be optimized piece by piece. [Audit results](experiments/local_foundations/audit_results.json), [CM results](experiments/collective_metrics/results.json)

PIT completed 210 full-chain comparisons and 2,100 stable-byte checks, but all 3 planted mislabeled timestamps were still admitted, and the future-append invariance check passed all the same. Of P2's 25 hand-picked semantic cells, only 3 pass under the width .10 gate and 22 are too wide. This is not a 12% acceptance rate on natural text, nor a rejection of P2 as a whole. The restricted XBRL parser handles only hand-made local formats; there is no real parsing rate and no historical time certificate. [PIT record](pit/results.json), [numeric record](numeric/results.json), [XBRL scope](xbrl/README.md)

**Audit-structure computations.** Among the initial 20 fixed-threshold cells, the direct method strictly improves on the strongest rectangle in 9 cells and gives no gain in 11, with median 0. At the representative point it is 88 vs 114, but a balanced total-count gate can also achieve 88. After the upgrade to the fixed total-count gate, of the same 20 cells, 14 beat the total-count gate and only 5 beat min(Box,TotalK), with a maximum saving of 1/11 and median 0. The 8 all-strata conjunction cells and the 16 star cells show no new gain over that simple combination. The other 4 sharp points have only computed analytic bounds; at N=32 the fixed-quota comparison can guarantee a saving of at least 44.54%, but this cannot be written up as exact optimality or as a real audit. In round 9, all of the complete 32 cells beat the total-count gate of the same randomized design class, but the 8 conjunctive points are only as good as the original single-stratum census. The representative hand-made population is N=32, d=2047, r=1024, 65,504 documents in total: the structural target needs at least 141 documents and the total-count gate at least 190. The 141 is achieved by a random census of 4 strata plus 13 more sampled documents; 140 documents do not satisfy α=.05. The saving is 49/190, about 25.79%. The old fixed direct method still has only the [1024,1118] bound and the old fixed simple combination the [2016,2047] bound; these should not be mixed into a like-for-like comparison. Analytically, along the r=N² sequence the two randomized budgets divided by N tend to 4.4 and 2log20 respectively, and the saving tends to about 26.56%; this is not a new empirical effect. ## 7. How the nine rounds changed the conclusions

| Round | Main progress | Corrections that must be kept |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Literature, claims audit, T series, synthetic foundation | Most components already exist; old results cannot be fully recovered from summaries; no evidence of a permission gain |
| 2 | CM collective statistics, audit frontier, raw-material inventory and path comparison | Non-additive statistics need joint optimization; real data exists but the premises are incomplete |
| 3 | IA impact structure and fixed-design optimization, source date check | Against the strongest joint rectangle, the median over the full 20 cells is 0; matching dates do not prove historical versions |
| 4 | Brand-new real entity-sentiment simple baseline | A real, reviewable starting point, but no proof that a complex model is necessary |
| 5 | PNI and the six PIT steps | Record non-interference is not real source compliance |
| 6 | SE semantic sets and strict numeric outer envelopes | Numerically exact can still be semantically very wide; rules can fully solve the direction task |
| 7 | Consolidated English manuscript and paper-level adversarial review | The total-count gate reproduces 88 and the rectangular order gap; general non-rectangular count sets are equivalent to the direct method |
| 8 | LV strongest fixed total-count gate and the boundary of structural value | Fixed simple combinations give no gain in most cells; another scale has a sharp constant gain, but the design-class restriction matters |
| 9 | RA matching lower bound for arbitrary randomized designs | Simple randomized whole-stratum audits attain the optimum; pooled sampling also gets the same-class total-count optimum; a constant gap remains in the fair comparison |

This trajectory is not a continual search for points where the original plan succeeds. It is a continual strengthening of the alternatives: first correcting the weak Bonferroni comparison, then adding the total-count gate and simple combinations, and finally removing the artificial fixed per-stratum quotas. The favorable structures 38 vs 48, 10 vs 11 and 44.54% are all kept, but they cannot replace the full tables, the different design classes and the real premises. Later exploratory positive results also do not retroactively make the original 20% pilot gate pass. [Index of the original per-round reports](INDEX.md)

## 8. Internal peer review: which objections changed the deliverable

The internal independent AI review pointed out the following issues and drove their correction: the 2k+1 exception caused by ties; metadata selection leakage; conflating fixed-seed non-interference with information measurability; seeds that are independent only of the marginal can leak the future through historical variables; misusing a sum for the rectangle risk weakens the baseline; per-class macro-F1 extremes are not necessarily jointly attainable; semantic branches need a shared target and fixed correspondence; overall parsing error cannot pass for post-acceptance risk; if a randomized audit may abstain when all findings are zero, the optimality degenerates. These are corrections to the conditions of the conclusions, not layout feedback. [Path review and responses](paths/REVIEW_RESPONSE.md), [index of topic reviews](INDEX.md)

The more important opposing view is "simple methods are enough". The sign rule, general non-rectangular safe count sets, the balanced total-count gate, single-stratum census and randomized whole-stratum designs removed, one by one, the necessity of the complex components originally proposed. In the end, the 11 proof obligations of RA passed local structural checks and an independent close reading; an independent document-level enumeration covering 709 budget cells and 128 risk checks against the main table support the implementation. The final existing reproduction record shows 125 unit tests and 13 commands passing, plus 10 source-parsing self-tests; this delivery did not rerun them. [Randomization review](reports/randomization_review.md), [existing reproduction log](reproduction_log.json)

These checks are not a Lean formalization, external human peer review or Fable's endorsement. Finding no counterexample does not prove that the real-world premises hold. Raw sources, real parsing coverage, model validity and novelty each still need their own evidence.

## 9. Decision gates and final states: the delivery is complete, the research goal is still not met

The original G0 required family-cluster intervals for the old 24M model together with three baselines on the same test set, a permission ablation, and a synthetic world into which contamination can be planted exactly. The last two have deliverables of explicit scope; the first still lacks the old row-level evidence and the FinBERT/small-LLM comparisons. The new entity-sentiment baseline cannot silently replace the old G0; accepting a protocol rebuilt on a brand-new task needs an explicit revision.

For G1, novelty is still partial, a complete non-empty certificate under real parameters is missing, and the main-line T4 budget has not been measured, so no flagship was chosen. There is no contaminated/clean dual training chain for an actual core backbone plus shard heads, no full-tuple parsing audit on natural text, and no complete comparison of P2 soft-constraint and hard-constraint models. Several rounds of mathematical optimality do not fill these gaps. [Completion audit](reports/completion_audit.md)

The four final states likewise cannot be merged and declared complete. The non-empty certificate plus checker on real data still lacks the full chain. An arXiv-style working draft exists, but the central contribution, real model evidence and novelty are not complete. The target top-level repository reorganization and the legacy migration are not complete. Old files are kept. The round 7 [English technical manuscript](paper/manuscript.md) and [reading PDF](paper/output/pdf/audit_budgets_working_report.pdf) are historical versions; they do not contain all the corrections from rounds 8 and 9, and they are not an accepted LaTeX compilation of the full notes.tex.

Values in the path gates such as width .10, 20% cost saving and 50% acceptance rate are pilot resource rules awaiting freezing. They are not mathematical constants, industry standards or standards Fable has agreed to. Real-data validity is a hard premise, and the threshold for scientific usefulness is a separate question; thresholds cannot be moved to confirm success after the fact.
